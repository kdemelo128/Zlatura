Underwriting
Template filler

Bring your rent roll, financials and leases into one reviewed model. Adjust the assumptions, explore the deal and fill your Excel template.

First time here? Start with these four steps
  1. Add your files. Start with the rent roll and operating statement. Add leases, plats, surveys and plans as supporting evidence.
  2. Review the evidence. Check document types, source totals and pages. Approve a drawing area or lease term before it changes a tenant.
  3. Set the assumptions. Choose comparison years and lease-up. Expand tenant and expense details only when you need them.
  4. Fill and download. Open “UW Read me” in Excel for the input-to-output map and editing instructions.

Missing documents do not block a partial model. Missing values remain visible for review.

Using the original Claude template

The supplied Claude Summary keeps its familiar format and links to the expanded UW model. Its tenant preview shows the first 20; the full UW rent roll and recovery detail include every tenant. The original income/expense, building-stack, rent-roll, pricing and ten-year cash-flow outputs are linked to this model. The complete rent-roll output expands for all tenants. Unused original inputs remain hidden. Attach a firm workbook to create reviewed links into its existing cells; unmapped values remain unchanged.

Every projection depends on reviewed inputs. Printed areas and dimension calculations remain separate; this tool does not infer rentable area from an unscaled picture. Check AI-extracted values against the source. In Excel, the Assumptions and Lease inputs tabs drive the formulas. Changes to tenant/account counts, classifications or displayed years require a fresh export.
