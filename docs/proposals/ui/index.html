The new look: wireframes

Static sketches for proposal 2b. They show layout, hierarchy and states, not final styling. Every name and number is invented; the chart numbers were worked out with the app's own engine for an invented deal (asking $6,200,000, NOI $403,000, 60% loan at 6.50%, 30-year amortization). Light and dark follow the device setting.

  • Home, phoneHeadline, Review, Today, Continue working, Pipeline, Tasks; the bottom bar with More
  • Home, desktopThe left rail (collapsible) and two columns
  • Deal page, phoneCompact header, three-number headline, section switcher, three charts
  • Customize modePresets, move up and down, Edit as a list, the Hidden shelf, accent and density, Reset
  • A chart while trying a rateThe scrub, the readout, Keep as a What-if scenario, and the table alternative
  • Addendum mockups (2026-10-29)Visual language, source marks, Underwrite cash flow, Documents, first-run tour, My workspace; phone and desktop, light and dark

Checked 2026-10-15 with axe-core 4.10.2 (WCAG 2.2 AA and best practices) in Chromium at 390 × 844 and 1440 × 900, light and dark, at the top of each page: no violations, except one on Customize at 1440 × 900, where two shelf buttons sit under the sticky bottom bar at that scroll position (target-size, "partially obscured"). Focused controls scroll clear of the bars (scroll padding); the build's axe run scrolls each target into view, and the bottom bar is a phone-only element there. Not checked: Safari, Firefox, a screen reader.
