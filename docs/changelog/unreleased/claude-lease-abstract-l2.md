# Read a lease PDF into the lease abstract

Reading a lease was typed by hand in the lease abstract. Now a "Read the lease" button on each linked document reads the PDF on the phone with fixed rules (no AI) and shows what it found before anything is written.

- **Reads text and scans.** Text PDFs are read directly; scanned pages are offered for OCR (the engine is loaded once for the run, a page that takes over a minute is stopped and listed, Cancel keeps the pages read and offers Resume).
- **Staged, then kept.** Each value shows its page and a short quote, or "Not found in pages 1-n", with a confidence word. "Keep selected" and "Keep all found" write it as the reader's, unticked, in one Undo step; the tick stays the broker's. A typed or ticked value is never overwritten.
- **Honest about what it cannot decide.** Relative dates ("90 days after delivery") and a month with no day stay unresolved, a rent unit comes only from the words, a monthly and annual figure that disagree are kept with both figures and a "Use the monthly figure" / "Use the annual figure" tap, "gross leasable area" asks "rentable or gross?", and "Year n" rows are counted only by a tap. An amendment layers by its effective date; the rent it replaces is kept, struck through.
- **Data format.** Schema 9 (`abstracts-read`, a marker only): items may carry `by: reader`, a confidence, the reader name and the quote's place. Older apps (4.7.96 and earlier) refuse a backup made after this, instead of turning reader values into "typed by the broker". The Documents checklist shows a read lease as read.
- **Review fixes.** Two rent tables with the same heading but different figures are one "needs your choice" term, never merged (the rent was counted twice). A number like 125.000 SF, 1.250,50 or 8,5% asks (thousands or decimal mark?) instead of being read as 125, and a decimal part is never dropped. A step an amendment replaces is shown struck through and left out of the term total, the annual rate, the exports' totals and the next-date list; an amendment whose dates only partly overlap an old step leaves it in place with a flag, and one that gives months only supersedes nothing and says so. A value read by OCR is never above medium confidence (the page average cannot vouch for one digit). A quote ends with its sentence, and its stored offsets map back to the page text. A replaced value's note names the document that said it.
- **Tested** with invented lease layouts and PDFs, an invented noisy scanned lease read by the real OCR engine in `leaseread-flow`, hostile backups, an old-backup fixture, and mutation checks. Saved-deal results and the 646 displayed figures are unchanged. Chromium only: OCR speed on a phone is not measured.
