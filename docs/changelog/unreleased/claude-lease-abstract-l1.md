# A Lease abstracts card on every deal: type a lease's terms into the firm's layout, tick each one against its page, and export it

Step L1 of `docs/proposals/lease-abstract.md` (design note `docs/proposals/lease-abstract-l1-note.md`; the data format approved: `deal.abstracts`, a silent migration step). **There is no reader, no OCR and no AI in L1:** you type or paste what the lease says, note the page, and tick each value. Nothing writes to the rent roll, the key dates or the credit record, and no deal figure reads it.

- **Lease abstracts card** on the deal page (Overview, under Documents), collapsed by default and remembered on this device. Each row shows the title, the tenant, the expiration, "n of m checked" and the open flags, with Open, Workbook, Print and More (CSV, JSON, Rename, Delete with Undo). A lease or amendment on the Documents card has an **Abstract** button that opens its abstract or makes one from the file name.
- **The editor** (a full-height sheet) has the firm's layout in our own words: parties and notices, premises, term and dates, money and recoveries, 24 numbered notes plus 16 you can add, your own notes, your own flags, rent terms with steps, and options. Each value has a visible label, a status in words (Unread, Found, Not found, Needs your choice, Checked), **Lease says none**, **Not in lease** and a **Checked** toggle. Source is a disclosure: the document, the page and a quote of at most 240 characters. Values are read when you leave a field; nothing redraws while you type. Keys: j and k move between fields, x ticks, / goes to the filter.
- **A blank is never a zero.** "Lease says none" stores 0, false or "None" and needs a page, a quote or the note "no clause"; "Not in lease" stores nothing and prints "Not found in lease"; clearing deletes the key.
- **Rent in whole cents.** A step keeps the amounts the lease wrote (monthly, annual, $/SF a year or a month) and which one is the rent; monthly is converted once. **Annual rate per SF = monthly x 12 / rentable SF**, and the screen and every file say so. A $/SF on usable, gross or land SF, or with no square feet, shows no total and is never multiplied. Steps written as "months 1 to 12" wait for a typed and ticked rent commencement and are never stored as dates.
- **Twelve checks** (monthly against annual; annual against $/SF; $/SF on a different basis; overlaps and gaps, from `lease.js`; date order; stated term against the dates; a relative date with no date; steps against the term; a pro-rata share with no "covers" note; a "none" with no evidence; a percent outside 0 to 100; an unexplained "needs your choice"). They never change a value; a problem stops the tick and says why. Editing a ticked item unticks it and announces why.
- **Files, all marked "contains lease wording":** an Excel workbook (one sheet per abstract; the portfolio adds a Summary sheet) in the firm's layout, with live formulas for the total rent, the annual rate and the weighted average, each stored with its own value; a CSV (every cell through the formula guard, no quotes); a JSON copy with the quotes; and a print view with a source column and a "Draft: n items not checked" footer. Typed text is only ever a plain text cell, never a formula or link. A confidential deal's files carry the CONFIDENTIAL marks.
- **Saved data:** `deal.abstracts` (schema 8, silent step `abstracts-init`, in backups, listed as not calculated). An untouched list is no edit for History. An older app refuses a backup holding it rather than dropping it.
- **Tested** with invented text only: `abstracts.test.js` (normaliser against hostile input, setters, each check with a pass and a trip at the boundaries, fast-check properties), `abstractexport.test.js` (the workbook read back, formulas only in the template cells, injection kept as strings, CSV and JSON), `migrate.test.js` on a new 4.7.88 backup, and `abstract-flow` (Chromium, iPhone size: typing one key at a time in every kind of input, the whole flow, LibreOffice recalculation of both workbooks, axe, 44 px targets, 150 ms a commit with 60 abstracts, a backup round trip, a confidential deal making no request). Saved results did not change: 618 saved deal results and 646 displayed figures are identical.
- **Not done in L1:** reading a lease, OCR, AI, "Use" into the rent roll or key dates, structured free rent, versions, reconciliation, Word and a JSON import. The owner's reference workbook was read for its structure only.
