# Clearer "Choose your next move", analyst toolkit and advanced scenarios in the underwriting tool

The tool's ten cards now sit under four plain headings (Work the numbers, Check the evidence, Build the model, Share the result), most used first, one "Start here" card in each, each with its one-line description and a longer "what this does" hint (on mouse hover and keyboard focus; on a touch tap it shows for a few seconds). The analyst toolkit and advanced scenarios keep all six tabs each, now under three headings with a one-line description under the tabs. Every button, select, text field and fold in the three areas is at least 44 px tall. The hint only fades (150 ms, opacity and transform) and does not animate under reduced motion. No control was removed or renamed and no calculation or stored value changed: the fictional example shows the same figures (checked by a hash of every displayed figure on the page and on all twelve tabs). New browser flow `uwclarity-flow` (31 checks, 19 of which fail on the old code), with axe inside the tool's frame. Edited directly in `app/uwtool/` (`tool-library.js`, `analyst-ui.js`, `advanced-ui.js`, new `clarity.js`, `restored.css`).
