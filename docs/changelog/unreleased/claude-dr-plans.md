# Two one-page plans: a stolen or lost laptop or phone, and disaster recovery

Docs only (F10). **`docs/security/stolen-laptop.md`** says what is on a device, the order to act in (withdraw the
device's AI token, change passwords, restore onto a new device) and what Zlatura does not protect (no encryption
beyond the operating system; backups unencrypted until F9). **`docs/security/disaster-recovery.md`** says where
the data lives (each device only), the backup habit, how to test a restore, and how to recover the app site and
the AI server, with the data-loss and recovery-time figures stated honestly. Both are linked from
`docs/security-checklist.md`. No app, engine or saved-result change.
