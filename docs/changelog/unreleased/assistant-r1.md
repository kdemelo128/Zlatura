# Monal, an assistant on your device: ask about the deal and see where each answer came from

A bird button now sits on every screen (not inside the full-screen Underwrite tool). It opens a panel (a bottom sheet on a phone) where a broker types a question and gets an answer worked out on the device from the open deal and the app's own engine. No language model, no new library, no network request, no change to the data format or to any saved result.

- **What it answers (release 1, 18 kinds).** Cap rate at a typed price; the price for a target cap rate; DSCR, debt service, loan amount, debt yield and the largest loan the deal supports; leases expiring by year (or all years); WALT; what is missing from the deal; what needs review (Data QC and the deal's own checks); how the deal compares to the comps; break-even occupancy; commission at a price from the deal's fee terms; where a figure came from; a figure of the deal (price, NOI, cap rate, price per SF or unit, occupancy, equity, cash flow, cash-on-cash, expense ratio); a summary; the site visit notes; a glossary of 13 terms; open a screen or section; add a follow-up task; what it can do. Questions are matched by rules and synonyms, with numbers and dates read by the app's own parsers (`parseNum`, `parsePct`, `parseDateText`).
- **Every figure comes from the shared engine** (`analyze`, `runScenario`, `scenarioAnswers`, `rentRollSummary`, `feeSummary`, `sizeLoan`, `breakEvenOccupancy`) or from stored deal data. There is no second copy of the math. Every answer states a year or a month and SF or units, and cites its source with a "Show where this came from" button that opens the field, the rent roll, the card, or the OM page the figure was read from.
- **It refuses to invent.** A blank input is reported as blank with a button to the field (never zero); a number with no k or m is asked about, not guessed; a question outside the set says plainly it cannot answer that yet and shows what it can do. Text in a deal note is shown as written, in quotes, and is never acted on.
- **Actions ask first.** A follow-up task shows its title, day and deal, waits for a yes, is added through the same `crm.js` path Home uses, and offers Undo.
- **The panel.** A modal dialog in both looks: focus goes into the question box and back to the bird, Escape closes, Tab wraps inside, answers are announced (polite live log), targets are 44 px, motion is off when asked. On a phone the bird sits bottom left so it never meets Home's floating primary button. Answers are text nodes only.
- **Tested.** 29 unit tests (a fixed question set with hand-worked answers on an invented deal, a refuses-to-invent set including a prompt-injection string in a note, a check that the assistant's files hold no network or HTML-writing call); the `assistant-flow` browser flow at phone and desktop size, light and dark, both looks (ask, cite, open the source, a blank to its field, a task with confirm and Undo, axe, zero requests while asking, 44 px targets, 130% text at 320 px, the 150 ms budget). Both fail on the code before. Saved results and displayed figures are unchanged (golden checks identical).
