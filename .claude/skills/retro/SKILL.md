# Retro

Read `docs/NOW.md`, `CHANGELOG.md` for the period, and the merged PRs' checks. Answer in at most 15 lines:
- **Went well:** what saved time or caught a bug (a check, a rule, a skill).
- **Cost us:** reruns, failed checks, blocked pushes, duplicated work, surprises. Give the cause, not the blame.
- **Numbers:** PRs merged, checks that failed first time, tokens or agents used if known.
- **One change at most per item:** a rule to add or drop, a check to add, a skill to trim. Propose it; changes to `CLAUDE.md` or the gates are the owner's to approve.
Keep it factual and short. Do not rewrite history or re-open decisions the owner already made.
